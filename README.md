# 🍝 Mensa-chan

> A local-first terminal assistant for everyday life.

Mensa-chan is an open-source terminal assistant focused on privacy, modularity and long-term maintainability.

Originally created as a cooking assistant, it is designed to grow into a modular personal companion capable of organizing recipes, writing projects, notes and everyday tasks without relying on cloud services.

Everything is local.
Everything belongs to you.


## Philosophy

Mensa-chan follows a few simple principles.

- 🏠 Local first
- 🔒 Privacy by design
- 🧩 Modular architecture
- ❤️ Long-term maintainability
- 🤝 Assist, don't replace


## Planned Modules

### 🍝 Cooking

Ingredient management, recipe suggestions and pantry organization.

### 📖 Writing

Writing projects, timelines, characters and notes.

### 📝 Notes

Quick capture, search and synchronization.

### 🛠 Utilities

Small tools that simplify everyday workflows.


## Roadmap

### v0.1 — Radicchio & Speck

The first release focuses on a single goal:

Given the ingredients available at home, suggest a meal that makes sense.


## Why?

Mensa-chan started after cooking a simple pasta with radicchio, speck, parmesan and black pepper while waiting for my mother to come home from work.

What began as a joke quickly evolved into the idea of building a local-first assistant designed to organize everyday life from the terminal.
